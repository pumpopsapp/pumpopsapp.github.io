LACoFD Policy — Hose Testing
AT A GLANCE
  • All fire hose is pressure tested and inventoried once a year, during February, following NFPA 1962 service test guidelines.
  • Company officers are responsible for conducting the annual test and surveying hose as needed.
  • Hose that fails visual inspection is never pressure tested — tag it, log it on Form 18, and send it to the hose shop.

Hydra-assist valves are never in the test layout — they can inadvertently pressurize the municipal water system.

RUNNING THE TEST
  • 1 · Inspect first. Check every length for mildew, rot, burns, cuts. Check couplings and replace missing or damaged gaskets. Failed lengths come out of the layout.
  • 2 · Lay out. Straight lines, no kinks or twists, max 300' per line, hose with the same test pressure grouped together. 3½" and larger tested lying on the ground, connected through a short 2½" section of equal or higher test pressure. Repaired or re-coupled hose: one length at a time. On a Department pumper, connect test lines on the side opposite the engineer's panel; use a certified hose test gate valve and gauge.
  • 3 · Fill at 45 PSI. Nozzle/test cap open, raise to about 45 PSI, bleed all air, close slowly. Holding 45: hand-tighten couplings only (no spanners), and mark each hose at the base of the coupling with permanent marker.
  • 4 · Raise & hold. Clear everyone to 15'. Slowly raise to the service test pressure and hold 5 minutes within ±5 PSI, hose test gate valve cracked. Inspect from a distance.
  • 5 · Evaluate. Shut down, equalize below 45 PSI, then check coupling marks. Any slippage, leak, or bulge = failure: tag at the failure area, remove from service, send to the hose shop. If a length fails mid-test, pull it and restart the test for the rest.
SAFETY — NO EXCEPTIONS
  • Remove all air before closing the line — entrapped air creates serious accident potential.
  • All personnel at least 15' from the sides and free ends of hose at test pressure.
  • Secure the shutoff device or the hose immediately behind it.
  • Relief valve in use and set no higher than the hose's service test pressure.
PAPERWORK — FORM 18
  • Complete the Annual Hose Inventory and Test Record (Form 18) after testing — one set per apparatus, separate sheet per hose size (mark HS, SS, or BP for hard suction, soft suction, bypass).
  • List section numbers oldest first; record manufacturer, year, and tester's initials.
  • Disposition codes: T + Form 47 # transferred · S + Form 47 # surveyed.
  • Surveyed hose also gets a Survey Request (Form 41).
  • Keep completed records in the station file for 5 years after all section numbers are crossed out.
BETWEEN TESTS
  • Run water through hose at least every 3 months (suction every 30 days) unless used in service.
  • Reload rubber hose after the annual test and at least quarterly, changing fold positions.
  • Clean with clear water only (mild soap OK on heavily soiled rubber); dry cotton hose thoroughly before loading. Rubber hose needs no drying.
