PUMPOPS - Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 6, 2026

The short version

PumpOps collects no data. Period.

Information we collect

None. PumpOps does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, usage data, analytics, location data, or identifiers of any kind.

Data stored on your device

Your saved hose lays, preset order, theme choice, and settings are stored only on your device, using your device's local app storage. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Network access

PumpOps works entirely offline and makes no network connections.

Third parties

PumpOps contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

Children

PumpOps does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of PumpOps ever changes these practices, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy: PumpOpsAPP@gmail.com
